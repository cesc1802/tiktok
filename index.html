Automation APP

Schedule and publish videos to your own TikTok account.

Automation APP is a tool that lets creators connect their TikTok account, upload videos, and publish or schedule them from one place. You sign in with TikTok, authorize only the permissions you choose, and can disconnect at any time.

How it works

  • Sign in with your TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit.
  • Upload a video and add a caption and privacy setting.
  • Publish now or schedule it; the app posts it to your account through the TikTok Content Posting API.
