Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Automation APP (the "App", "we", "us", or "our") collects when you use it, how we use and share that information, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

In short: we only access the TikTok data you authorize, we use it only to upload, publish, and show your own content, we never sell it, and you can disconnect and have it deleted at any time.

1. Information we collect

a. Information from TikTok

When you sign in with TikTok, TikTok asks you to approve specific permissions ("scopes"). Depending on what you approve, we may receive:

TikTok scopeDataWhy we use it
user.info.basic Open ID, display name, avatar Identify your account and show who is signed in
user.info.profile / user.info.stats Profile link, bio, follower and video counts Show your profile summary in the dashboard
video.upload Permission to send videos to your TikTok drafts Upload videos you choose so you can finish posting in TikTok
video.publish Permission to post videos to your profile Publish or schedule videos only when you ask us to
video.list Your public videos and basic metadata Show the status and history of posts made through the App

We also receive OAuth access and refresh tokens, which let the App act on your behalf within the scopes you approved. We never receive or store your TikTok password.

b. Content you provide

Videos, captions, hashtags, privacy settings, and scheduling times that you enter into the App.

c. Technical information

Basic logs needed to run and secure the service, such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, and error reports.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your account connected;
  • To upload, publish, and schedule the content you choose to your TikTok account;
  • To show you your profile summary and the status of your posts;
  • To maintain, secure, debug, and improve the App;
  • To respond to your requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning models, and we do not combine it with data from other sources to build profiles of you.

3. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With TikTok, to carry out the actions you request, such as publishing a video;
  • With service providers (for example, cloud hosting and storage) that process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations and only as needed to run the App;
  • For legal reasons, if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users, us, or others;
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, subject to this Policy.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • Uploaded video files are kept only until they are successfully sent to TikTok (or the scheduled post is cancelled), and are then deleted from our storage within 30 days.
  • Account data and access tokens are kept while your TikTok account stays connected.
  • When you disconnect your account or ask us to delete your data, we delete your tokens, profile data, and stored content within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records to comply with law.

You can also revoke the App's access directly in TikTok at any time: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Once access is revoked, we can no longer retrieve any data from your account.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), encryption of access tokens at rest, and restricted access to production systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we take steps to ensure that such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards.

9. Third-party services

Your use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of TikTok or other third-party services.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above. If a change is material, we will give reasonable notice in the App.
